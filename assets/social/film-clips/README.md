# Prepared film clips

The initial 17 prepared excerpts were imported from `RLE-Bench_Editable_Project.zip`.
They are edited footage for the RLE-Bench film project, not the final rendered
research film or teaser. The archive does not contain those final MP4s.

All 11 source videos already exist under `assets/blog/demos/` and were not
copied again. No homepage or blog embeds are changed by this import.
The initial import omitted the film renderer, soundtrack, and other project files.

`import-manifest.json` records each imported archive path, byte count, SHA-256,
existing source-video locations, and the original editing manifest. Original
editing-manifest paths refer to the supplied project layout; the source-video
mapping identifies their locations in this repository.

Prepared clips may contain crops, resizing, and additional playback-rate
changes. Consult the editing manifest and source video annotations when reusing
them; demonstration footage does not establish aggregate benchmark performance.
