A Qualifying Exam for Coding Agents
as Robot Learning Engineers

RLE-Bench evaluates whether general-purpose coding agents can solve robot learning engineering problems through observation, experimentation, and iterative development. The benchmark spans four aspects: interactive control, policy development, perception and estimation, and mechanical design. Agents work under task-specific time, interaction, and compute budgets, and are evaluated independently under hidden physical conditions.
